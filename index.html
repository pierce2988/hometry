Credit Tips
💳
Keep Utilization Below 30%
Utilization is 30% of your score. Paying below 30% of your limit can add 20–40 points quickly.
📅
Never Miss a Payment
Payment history is 35% of your score. Set autopay on every account at least 6 months before applying.
🚫
Avoid New Credit Accounts
Each hard inquiry can drop your score 5–10 points. Avoid new credit 6 months before your mortgage.
🏦
Build Your Reserves
Lenders want 2–6 months of payments in savings after closing. It signals stability and reduces your risk.
