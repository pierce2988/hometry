📊 Which score should I use?
Free apps and mortgage lenders don't always show the same number. Here's how to give your best estimate.
FICO vs. VantageScore

Mortgage lenders almost always use a FICO score. Many free apps show a VantageScore instead — a different model that can read higher or lower than the score a lender pulls.

Why Credit Karma may look different

Credit Karma and similar apps typically display VantageScore 3.0. Don't be surprised if your mortgage FICO comes back 20–50 points different in either direction — that's normal.

What to enter here

Enter your best estimate of your FICO score. If a free app is all you have, use that number as a starting point — you can refine it anytime.

Why it matters

A closer estimate means a more accurate Mortgage Readiness Score and more realistic payment estimates — so the plan you get actually fits you.
